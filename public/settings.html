Split timing

Pool length

Splits are clocked from one end of the pool, so each split covers two lengths (50m in a 25m pool, 100m in a 50m pool).

A second split on the same lane within this window is ignored and logged. Allowed range: 1 to 60.

Changing these settings during a running heat is not recommended.
